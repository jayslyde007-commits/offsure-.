    Questions

    Frequently asked

    Where do I find my tracking number?

    It is on your booking confirmation and on the waybill attached to your parcel. Tracking numbers start with "OFS".

    How often is tracking updated?

    Every time your shipment passes a checkpoint such as pickup, a hub, customs or the delivery vehicle.

    My tracking number is not found. What now?

    New numbers can take a few hours to appear. Check for typos, then contact our support team if it still does not show.

    Can I change the delivery address?

    Yes, before the shipment is out for delivery. Contact us with your tracking number and the new address.
